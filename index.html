What you'll cover

No theory for the sake of theory. Grounded in real examples, good and bad.

01 · Foundations

What digital accessibility actually is

What WCAG principles mean in practice — and why they apply to marketing and communications work, not just development.

02 · Your users

How limitations affect the way people use digital products

How different visual, hearing, motor, cognitive and perceptual limitations affect digital use — and what that means for how you create content.

03 · Real examples

Good solutions and bad ones, side by side

Contrast, buttons, links, forms, image descriptions, video subtitles, documents, language and structure — shown clearly, with the reasoning behind each.

04 · Before you publish

Catch issues before they become problems

A practical checklist of questions to ask before content goes live.

05 · Risk & responsibility

What non-compliance means for your organisation

Legal, financial and reputational implications — clearly explained, without the jargon.

06 · The upside

Accessibility makes you easier to find — and harder to ignore

Well-structured, accessible content ranks better in search, gets picked up by AI tools navigating the web on users' behalf, and converts more of the audience you already have.

This course is for you if you work with digital content - writing it, designing it, ordering it or signing it off. Don't worry about the technical side of accessibility - you only need to know what to look for, what to ask, and flag when something isn't right.
